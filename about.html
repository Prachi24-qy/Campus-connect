About CampusConnect

Connecting students with campus information, announcements and opportunities.

Who We Are

CampusConnect is a student-friendly campus information platform designed to make college communication easier. It brings important notices, announcements and updates together in one place.

Our Mission

To make campus communication simple, accessible and convenient for every student.

Our Vision

To create a connected campus where students never miss important information and opportunities.

Our Key Features

📢 Notice Board

Access important college announcements in one place.

🔍 Smart Search

Find specific notices quickly using the search feature.

♻️ Restore Notices

Restore notices that have been moved to the deleted section.

📱 Easy Access

Use a simple and responsive interface across devices.

How It Works

  1. Visit the CampusConnect homepage.
  2. Browse the latest campus announcements.
  3. Use the search bar to find specific notices.
  4. Contact us if you have questions or suggestions.

Why Choose CampusConnect?

CampusConnect aims to reduce communication gaps and help students stay informed. Our goal is to make campus information easier to find and understand.
